Eigenvalues of a cylindrical shell conveying axial flow

The interactive companion of An Isogeometric Approach to the Flow-Induced Vibration of Cylindrical Shells Conveying Internal Axial Flow (Tunca & Uğurlu).

Each page draws the eigenvalues of one family of modes, the modes of one circumferential wavenumber n, against the flow velocity, as in the diagrams of the paper. A slider moves over the 328 velocity stations. At each station, every mode has a window with the axial profile of its radial displacement over one period and its axial composition. The 3D button of a window draws its mode on the shell, moving through one period. The paper shows the family n = 5; the pages show n = 4 to 7 on both far fields.
